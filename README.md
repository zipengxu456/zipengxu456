## Selected Collaborative Work

### [EXPoly](http://github.com/DaZhongLv/EXPoly)

A collaborative project connecting experimental 3D microstructures with molecular-dynamics simulations to investigate grain growth.

My role focused on experimental validation and comparison of simulated and measured microstructure evolution. I worked with the simulation and workflow developer to interpret discrepancies and assess how well the simulations captured experimentally observed behavior.

Related publication: [Comparing Molecular Dynamics Simulations of Grain Growth with Experimental Data](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4827552)
